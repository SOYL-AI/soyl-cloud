# Front Office and Reservations SOP

Sanchari Hotels — applies to Sanchari Riverside (Kochi), Sanchari Heights (Munnar)
and Sanchari Residency (Bengaluru). Effective 1 April 2026. Supersedes the
edition of 1 April 2024.

## 1. Reservation intake

All reservations are created in the property management system at the time of
enquiry. A booking is not considered held until it carries a confirmation
number generated by the system; a verbal assurance from any member of staff
does not create a held booking, and staff must not tell a caller that a room is
"kept aside" without generating one.

Every reservation record must carry the guest name as it appears on the
identity document to be presented at arrival, a contactable telephone number, an
email address where one is offered, the arrival and departure dates, the number
of adults and children, and the rate plan under which the room is sold. Where
the booking originates from a travel agent or an online channel, the channel
reference is stored against the record so the commission can be reconciled at
month end.

Reservations taken over the telephone are read back to the caller in full
before the confirmation number is issued. Reservations received by email are
confirmed in writing within four working hours during the day and by 10:00 the
following morning if received overnight. An enquiry that cannot be met is
answered with the nearest available alternative rather than a bare refusal, and
the refusal is recorded so that denied demand can be reviewed against the rate
strategy each month.

## 2. Identity, registration and foreign nationals

Every arriving guest presents a valid photo identity document at registration.
Indian nationals may present any government-issued photo identity other than a
photocopy. The document is verified against the person in front of the desk,
scanned into the guest record, and returned immediately; original documents are
never retained at the desk or held overnight against payment.

Foreign nationals must present a passport and a valid visa. The passport
biographical page, the visa page and the page bearing the most recent Indian
immigration arrival stamp are scanned. Form C is submitted electronically to
the immigration authority within twenty-four hours of arrival for every foreign
national, without exception and regardless of the length of stay. A guest who
declines to present a passport cannot be registered, and the duty manager is
called before the guest is refused.

Guests aged under eighteen may not be registered as the sole occupant of a
room. Where a room is occupied by more than one adult, every adult occupant is
registered individually, and the additional occupants present identity
documents in the same way as the person paying.

## 3. Arrival timings and early arrival

Standard arrival time is 14:00. A guest arriving before 14:00 is accommodated
where a serviced room of the booked category is available, at no charge. Where
no serviced room is available, luggage is taken into the left luggage room
against a numbered tag and the guest is directed to the lobby lounge, where tea
and coffee are complimentary while they wait.

Guaranteed occupancy of the room before 14:00 must be purchased. A room
released from 08:00 is charged at fifty per cent of the room-only rate for the
night; a room released from midnight of the preceding night is charged as a
full additional night. The charge is quoted before it is applied and is never
added silently to the folio.

Arrivals expected after 22:00 are flagged on the arrivals list at the morning
briefing so that the night team is prepared and the room is not released. Where
a guest has not arrived and cannot be reached by 08:00 the following morning,
the reservation is treated as a no-show under section 6.

## 4. Departure timings and late departure

Standard departure time is 11:00. A guest may request departure up to 13:00 at
no charge, and this is granted wherever the arrivals position allows. Beyond
13:00 the room is charged at fifty per cent of the room-only rate up to 18:00,
and as a full additional night after 18:00.

Requests for extended occupancy are decided by the duty manager against the
arrivals position for that night, not by the desk agent, and the decision is
recorded on the folio with the name of the manager who made it. On a date where
the property is fully committed, extended occupancy is declined and the guest is
offered use of the health club changing rooms and the left luggage room without
charge until 18:00.

Folios are presented for review before payment is taken. Where a guest disputes
a charge at departure, the disputed line is held and the remainder settled; the
dispute is passed to the duty manager the same day and answered within
forty-eight hours. Staff at the desk do not remove charges on their own
authority.

## 5. Cancellation and no-show — individual bookings

An individual booking made on a flexible rate plan may be withdrawn without
charge up to 18:00 on the day before arrival, measured in local time at the
property. A withdrawal received after that point, and a guest who does not
arrive, is charged one night's room and applicable tax.

Bookings made on an advance purchase rate plan are paid in full at the time of
booking and no part of that payment is returned if the guest withdraws, changes
the dates, or does not arrive. This is stated on the booking confirmation and
read aloud to a caller before the payment is taken.

Where a guest cannot travel because of a death or serious illness in the
immediate family, a natural disaster, or the cancellation of a scheduled flight
by the carrier, the charge is waived on the authority of the general manager
regardless of the rate plan. Documentary evidence is requested but the waiver is
not withheld pending it where the circumstances are evident.

A booking withdrawn by the property rather than the guest is never charged, and
the guest is placed in comparable accommodation at our cost under section 8.

## 6. Cancellation and no-show — corporate and contracted bookings

A room booked under a corporate contracted rate may be withdrawn without charge
up to 48 hours before the arrival date. Inside 48 hours, and for a guest who
does not arrive, one night's room and applicable tax is charged to the
company's account.

The 48-hour window is measured from 14:00 on the arrival date, not from the
moment of booking and not from midnight. A withdrawal notified at 15:00 two
days before arrival is therefore inside the window and is charged, which is
counter-intuitive and is the most common source of dispute; desk staff quote
the exact deadline date and time when the booking is made.

Where a company's contract specifies a different window, the contract prevails
over this section. The contracted terms are visible on the rate plan record in
the property management system and staff check them before quoting a deadline.

Charges raised against a corporate account are notified to the company's
nominated contact by email on the day they are raised. A charge that has not
been notified within seven days is written off rather than pursued, because a
charge a company first learns of on an invoice at month end costs more in
relationship than it recovers in revenue.

## 7. Group and block bookings

A block of five or more rooms on the same dates is handled as a group booking
and is contracted in writing rather than confirmed by email. The contract
records the room count by category, the rate, the release date, the attrition
allowance and the payment schedule.

Rooms held in a block that have not been named by the release date are returned
to general sale automatically. The release date is 30 days before arrival for
blocks of fewer than twenty rooms and 45 days for larger blocks, unless the
contract sets a different date.

Attrition is permitted up to twenty per cent of the contracted room nights
without charge. Reductions beyond twenty per cent are charged at eighty per cent
of the contracted rate for the rooms withdrawn. The allowance is calculated on
total room nights across the stay, not on the peak night, so a group that
shortens its stay consumes the allowance in the same way as one that reduces
its room count.

A deposit of twenty-five per cent of the contracted value is payable on signature
and is not returned if the group does not travel. The balance is payable on
presentation of the final invoice, within the credit terms agreed for the
account.

## 8. Overbooking and relocating a guest

The property may be sold beyond its physical capacity where the forecast
no-show rate supports it, on the authority of the revenue manager and never by
the desk. Where more guests arrive than there are rooms, the guest to be
relocated is chosen in this order: the shortest stay first, then the lowest
rate, then the most recent booking. Guests travelling with children, guests with
reduced mobility, guests on a corporate contract, and repeat guests are not
relocated.

A relocated guest is placed in a hotel of equal or better standard within eight
kilometres. We pay the first night's accommodation in full, the transport there
and back, and one telephone call. The guest is offered a return to our property
for the remainder of the stay at the rate they booked, upgraded by one category.

The duty manager speaks to the guest personally. The conversation begins with
what we will do rather than with an explanation of why the situation arose. A
relocation is recorded with the guest's name, the reason, and the cost, and the
list is reviewed by the general manager weekly.

## 9. Payment, deposits and card authorisation

A card presented at check-in is authorised for the full accommodation value plus
three thousand rupees per night against incidental spending. The authorisation
is explained to the guest as a hold rather than a charge, and the amount is
stated. Authorisations are released at departure on the same day.

Cash settlement requires a deposit of the full accommodation value plus five
thousand rupees per night, taken at check-in. Cash deposits are receipted and
the receipt is required before the balance is returned.

Charges are not posted to a room account without a signature or a registered
mobile authorisation from the occupant. A guest may nominate another party to
settle their account only in writing, and the nominated party's own card is
authorised separately.

Company accounts are settled against a purchase order or a signed letter of
authority held on file before arrival. Where neither is held, the guest settles
personally and reclaims from their employer; staff do not extend credit at the
desk.

## 10. Handling a complaint at the desk

A guest raising a complaint is heard without interruption and without an
explanation being offered before the account is complete. The room number, the
time and the substance of the complaint are recorded in the guest incident log
at the moment it is taken, not at the end of the shift.

Most matters are resolved by the duty manager within the same shift. Anything
not resolved within fifteen minutes is escalated to the general manager on
duty. A complaint concerning safety, food, or the conduct of a member of staff
is escalated immediately regardless of how quickly it might otherwise be
settled.

Compensation is offered only after the facts have been established, and never
as a way of ending a conversation. The duty manager may waive up to five
thousand rupees on their own authority; anything beyond that is authorised by
the general manager. What was done and what it cost is recorded against the
incident.

A guest who has complained is contacted before departure by the duty manager,
and the outcome is recorded whether or not the guest was satisfied.

## 11. Wake-up calls, messages and guest requests

Wake-up calls are entered into the system at the time they are requested and
repeated back to the guest. The system places the call and a member of the night
team confirms by telephone that the guest has answered. Where there is no
answer at the second attempt, a member of staff attends the room in person.

Messages for a resident guest are delivered to the room within fifteen minutes
of receipt and are also recorded in the guest record. Messages for a guest who
has not yet arrived are attached to the reservation and given at check-in.
Message content is never read aloud in the lobby or repeated to anyone other
than the addressee.

Requests made to the desk are logged and given a target time at the moment they
are taken, and the guest is told what that time is. A request that will take
longer than the target is reported to the guest before the target passes rather
than after it.

## 12. Key card issue and room access

A key card is issued only to a registered occupant of the room, on presentation
of identity, and never on the strength of a room number and a name alone. A
replacement for a lost card cancels the previous card at the moment it is
issued.

A guest requesting access to a room they are not registered in is refused, and
the refusal is not negotiable at the desk. Where the registered occupant is
present and wishes to authorise access, they attend the desk in person.

Master keys are signed out at the start of a shift against the holder's name
and signed back in at the end. A master key that cannot be accounted for at the
end of a shift is reported to the duty manager immediately and the affected
locks are rekeyed the same day.

Housekeeping, engineering and room service staff enter an occupied room only
after knocking, announcing themselves, and waiting. A room displaying a privacy
request is not entered; the procedure in the housekeeping standard applies.
