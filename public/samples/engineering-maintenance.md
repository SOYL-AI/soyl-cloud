# Engineering and Maintenance Manual

Sanchari Hotels — all properties. Effective 1 April 2026.

## 1. Planned preventive maintenance

Every item of plant carries a maintenance schedule in the asset register, and
the register is the single record of what exists, where it is, when it was last
attended to and when it is next due. Equipment not in the register is not
maintained, so anything installed or replaced is entered on the day it is
commissioned rather than at the next audit.

Preventive work is scheduled monthly and issued as a work order per asset.
Completion is recorded against the work order with the reading taken, the parts
consumed and the time spent. A work order closed without a reading is reopened
by the chief engineer.

Guest rooms receive a full preventive inspection quarterly, coordinated with the
housekeeping deep clean so that a room is taken out of service once rather than
twice. The inspection covers the air conditioning, the plumbing, the electrical
points, the lighting, the door and its closer, the lock, the safe, the
television, the curtains and their mechanism, and the balcony rail where fitted.

Deferred work is recorded with the reason and a date, and the deferred list is
reviewed by the general manager monthly. Work deferred twice is escalated rather
than deferred a third time.

## 2. Guest fault reporting and response

A fault reported by a guest is logged at the moment it is taken, with the room
number, the time and the description in the guest's own words. The response
target is fifteen minutes for attendance and the guest is told that target when
they report.

Faults are classified on attendance. A fault affecting safety, security, water,
power or air conditioning is attended immediately at any hour. A fault affecting
comfort or convenience is attended within two hours during the day and at
07:00 where it is reported after 23:00 and the guest is content to wait.

Where a fault cannot be resolved within two hours, the duty manager offers the
guest a move to another room. The offer is made rather than waited for. Where
the guest declines, the fault is re-attended and the guest updated every two
hours until it is resolved.

A room with an unresolved fault is not sold to the next arrival. The room is
placed out of order in the system by the engineer, not by the desk, and is
returned to sale by the engineer after the fault has been verified as fixed.

## 3. Air conditioning and ventilation

Room air conditioning filters are cleaned monthly and replaced at each quarterly
inspection. Condensate trays and drain lines are cleared at every filter clean,
being the most common cause of a ceiling stain in the room below.

Chiller performance is logged daily at 07:00 and 19:00: supply and return
temperature, pressure, and running hours. A drift of more than two degrees
between consecutive readings is investigated the same day rather than at the
next service.

Air handling units serving public areas are inspected weekly and the belts,
bearings and filters are checked. Fresh air dampers are verified open; a damper
closed to save energy produces a complaint about stuffiness that is never
attributed to its actual cause.

Cooling towers are dosed and bled according to the water treatment regime in
section 7, and are cleaned and disinfected twice yearly with the certificate
held by the chief engineer. Legionella risk assessment is reviewed annually and
after any modification to the system.

## 4. Water supply, hot water and plumbing

Hot water is delivered to guest outlets at not less than 50°C and not more than
55°C, measured at the tap. The calorifier stores at 60°C or above, and the
return leg is verified above 50°C weekly at the furthest point from the plant.

Temperatures are recorded weekly at a rotating sample of six rooms covering the
top floor, the bottom floor and the ends of each run, so that the sample reaches
the points where a system fails first.

A report of no hot water is attended immediately regardless of the hour and is
never held to the morning. Where the fault is in the plant rather than the
room, every occupied room is checked and the guests are informed before they
discover it themselves.

Water storage tanks are cleaned and disinfected every six months and the
certificate is held with the asset register. Potable water is tested quarterly
by an accredited laboratory for microbiological and chemical parameters, and the
result is retained for three years.

## 5. Electrical distribution and standby power

The main panel, distribution boards and earthing are inspected annually by a
licensed contractor, and thermographic scanning of the main and sub panels is
carried out annually with the report held by the chief engineer.

The diesel generator is test-run on load for thirty minutes every week and the
result recorded: starting time, voltage, frequency, load taken, oil pressure,
coolant temperature and fuel consumed. A generator tested off load is not
tested, because the fault that matters appears only under load.

Fuel is held at not less than seventy per cent of tank capacity at all times and
is checked for water and sediment monthly. The changeover time from mains
failure to generator supply is measured at each weekly test and must not exceed
fifteen seconds.

The uninterruptible supply serving the property management system, the network
and the emergency lighting is tested monthly for autonomy and the batteries are
replaced at the manufacturer's interval rather than on failure.

## 6. Lift maintenance and entrapment

Lifts are maintained under contract with a monthly service visit and a statutory
inspection at the interval set by the state lift authority. The service record
and the current licence are displayed in the machine room.

The emergency communication in every lift car is tested weekly by a member of
the engineering team, who presses the alarm and confirms it is answered at the
security desk and that two-way speech is clear. The test is recorded.

On an entrapment, the security desk speaks to the trapped passengers
continuously from the moment the alarm is received and does not leave the line
to fetch anyone. The lift contractor is called immediately. **No member of our
staff attempts a manual release unless they hold the contractor's certification
for that lift**, because a release attempted by an untrained person is how an
entrapment becomes a fall.

Passengers released are seen by the duty manager, offered water and a seat, and
asked whether they need medical attention. The entrapment is recorded and
reported to the lift contractor in writing the same day, and a lift with two
entrapments in a month is taken out of service pending investigation.

## 7. Water treatment and sewage

Cooling tower and boiler water are dosed and tested to the regime set by the
water treatment contractor, with conductivity, pH and biocide residual recorded
three times weekly. Readings outside range are corrected the same day and the
correction recorded.

The sewage treatment plant is operated continuously and monitored daily for
inlet and outlet flow, dissolved oxygen, and the appearance and settleability of
the sludge. Treated water is tested monthly by an accredited laboratory against
the consent conditions and the results retained for five years.

Treated water is reused for landscape irrigation and cooling tower make-up
where the quality permits, and never for any guest-facing outlet regardless of
the test result.

An odour complaint traced to the plant is treated as an operational failure of
the plant rather than a nuisance to be masked, and the corrective action is
recorded against the plant log.

## 8. Fire systems testing and maintenance

The fire alarm panel is checked daily for fault and disablement conditions and
the check is signed. A different call point is tested weekly on a rotation so
that every call point is tested within the year, and the weekly test is
announced in advance to avoid an unintended evacuation.

Detectors are cleaned and tested annually by the contracted service provider.
Emergency lighting is function-tested monthly and duration-tested annually for
the full rated period, with any luminaire failing the duration test replaced
rather than recharged.

Fire extinguishers are inspected monthly for pressure, seal and accessibility,
and are serviced annually by a licensed agency. Hose reels and hydrants are run
quarterly and the pressure at the topmost hydrant recorded. The fire pump —
main, standby and jockey — is test-run weekly under auto-start conditions.

Sprinkler control valves are verified open and locked weekly. A valve found
closed is opened and the event reported to the chief engineer and the general
manager the same day, whatever the reason given for its being closed.

## 9. Swimming pool operation

Free chlorine is maintained between 1 and 3 mg per litre and pH between 7.2 and
7.8, tested three times daily before opening, at midday and before closing, and
recorded on the pool log displayed at the poolside.

The pool is closed immediately and not reopened until parameters return to range
if free chlorine falls below 1 mg per litre, if pH is outside range, if the
water is not clear to the bottom at the deepest point, or on any faecal or
vomit contamination. A contamination event requires the published disinfection
procedure and the pool remains closed for its full duration regardless of
occupancy pressure.

Filters are backwashed at the interval set by the differential pressure reading
rather than on a fixed day, and the backwash is recorded. Water is tested
monthly by an accredited laboratory for microbiological parameters.

Depth markings, the no-diving notice, the rescue pole and the ring buoy are
verified present and legible daily. The pool operates from 06:00 to 20:00 and is
not available outside those hours, whether or not a lifeguard is present.

## 10. Energy and water consumption

Electricity, water, diesel and gas consumption are read daily at the same hour
and recorded per unit and per occupied room. The per-occupied-room figure is the
number reviewed, because total consumption falls with occupancy and conceals a
worsening efficiency.

Sub-meters serve the kitchen, the laundry, the chillers, the lifts and the guest
floors, so that a rise can be located rather than merely observed. A month-on-
month rise beyond five per cent per occupied room is investigated and the
finding recorded.

Guest room air conditioning is interlocked to the balcony door and to key card
occupancy. The interlock is verified at the quarterly room inspection, and a
defeated interlock is treated as a fault rather than as a guest preference.

Lighting in public areas runs to a published schedule with astronomical
switching. Manual overrides expire at the next scheduled change rather than
persisting, so that an override made for an event does not become permanent.

## 11. Contractor access and permits to work

A contractor attends only against a work order and signs in at the security
desk, presenting photo identity and, where the work requires it, evidence of
certification. Contractor identity is retained at the desk and returned on
sign-out.

Hot work — welding, cutting, grinding, or any work producing a flame or spark —
requires a permit issued by the chief engineer, valid for one shift only. The
permit requires the area to be cleared of combustibles, a fire extinguisher and
a fire watch present, the local detection zone isolated with the isolation
recorded, and a further inspection of the area one hour after the work finishes.

Work at height, work in a confined space, and work on live electrical apparatus
each require their own permit and are never carried out by a lone worker.

The detection zone isolated for hot work is restored at the end of the shift by
the person who isolated it, and the panel is verified clear of disablements at
the daily check in section 8. An isolation left in place overnight is the single
most dangerous condition on the property.

## 12. Spares, tools and inventory

Critical spares — pump seals, contactors, thermostats, door closers, lock
cylinders, lamp types in use, and the filter sizes for every air handling unit —
are held at a minimum stock level recorded in the asset register, and the level
is reviewed annually against consumption.

Spares are issued against a work order and the issue is recorded, so that
consumption per asset is visible and a component consuming spares faster than
its peers is identified before it fails.

Tools are signed out and back at the workshop. A tool taken into a guest room is
counted out and counted back before the engineer leaves the room, and a tool
unaccounted for is searched for before the room is released.

Chemicals held by engineering are stored in the locked plant store, never in a
guest-accessible area, and are subject to the same labelling and safety data
sheet requirements as the housekeeping chemical store.
