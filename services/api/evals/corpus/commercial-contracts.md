# Commercial Policy: Rates, Contracts and Procurement

Sanchari Hotels — all properties. Effective 1 April 2026.

## 1. Rate plans and rate codes

Every rate sold carries a code in the property management system, and no room is
sold at a rate without one. The code determines the inclusions, the deposit
requirement, the withdrawal terms and the commission treatment, so a room sold
under the wrong code is mispriced in every one of those respects.

The published codes are BAR for the flexible best available rate, ADV for the
advance purchase rate, COR for a contracted corporate rate, GOV for the
government rate, GRP for a contracted group rate, LNR for a long-stay negotiated
rate of seven nights or more, CMP for a complimentary room, and HSE for a house
use room occupied by staff or a contractor.

BAR is room only. ADV is room only, paid in full at booking, and is not
refundable or changeable. COR is room with breakfast unless the contract states
otherwise. GOV is room with breakfast at the rate notified by the state
authority and requires a government identity card at check-in.

CMP and HSE rooms are authorised by the general manager only, are recorded with
the reason and the authorising name, and are reported monthly with the revenue
displaced.

## 2. Rate parity and distribution

The rate offered on our own website is never higher than the rate offered on any
third-party channel for the same room, the same date and the same conditions.
Where a channel discounts below its contracted rate, the revenue manager
notifies the channel in writing the same day and the breach is recorded.

Our own website may offer inclusions that a third-party channel does not — a
free upgrade at check-in, a late departure, a food and beverage credit — because
value added is not a rate difference and does not breach parity.

Inventory is not withheld from a channel selectively as a commercial tactic.
Where a channel is closed, it is closed for a stated commercial reason recorded
by the revenue manager, and the same closure applies to every channel of that
type.

Rates are loaded to every channel by the revenue manager and verified on each
channel's own display before the load is signed off, because a rate loaded and
never seen is a rate nobody has checked.

## 3. Corporate contracted rates

A corporate rate is contracted annually, from 1 April to 31 March, against a
committed room-night volume. The contract records the rate by room category, the
inclusions, the withdrawal window, the payment terms, the volume commitment and
the review point.

Rates are set against the volume actually delivered in the preceding year, not
against the volume promised. A company that delivered below eighty per cent of
its commitment is renegotiated at the review point rather than renewed
automatically.

The standard withdrawal window is 48 hours before arrival, matching the front
office standard. A window different from the standard is recorded on the rate
plan in the property management system at the point of contracting, so that the
desk quotes the contract rather than the standard.

Blackout dates are stated in the contract and are limited to no more than
fifteen nights a year. A contracted rate is honoured on every other date,
including dates on which the property is in high demand, because a contracted
rate withdrawn when it is most needed is not a contract.

## 4. Group, conference and MICE pricing

Group business is quoted against the displacement it causes rather than against
a discount off the published rate. The quote accounts for the transient business
displaced at the forecast rate, the food and beverage contribution, the meeting
room hire and the ancillary spend.

The minimum acceptable total revenue per available room for group business is
set quarterly by the revenue manager and is not disclosed outside the commercial
team. A quote below the minimum requires the general manager's approval and the
reason is recorded.

Group rates are quoted net of commission where an agent is involved, and the
commission is stated separately in the contract rather than absorbed into the
rate.

A group holding meeting space without a room block is quoted at the published
space hire plus a minimum spend on food and beverage. Space is not held without
a signed contract and a deposit, whatever the strength of the relationship, and
a verbal hold expires after seven days.

## 5. Discount and approval authority

The duty manager may reduce a rate by up to ten per cent at the point of sale
without further approval, and may waive up to five thousand rupees against a
guest complaint under the front office standard.

The front office manager may approve up to twenty per cent. The revenue manager
may approve up to thirty per cent and may set a rate below the published floor
for a stated period. The general manager may approve any discount and is the
only authority for a complimentary room.

Every discount is recorded against the folio with the code, the approver and the
reason. A discount without a recorded reason is reversed at the month-end
review and charged to the department that granted it.

Authority does not pass down when a person is absent. Where the approver is not
available, the request goes up, never sideways, and never to the person who
would otherwise report to them.

## 6. Travel agent commission

Commission is ten per cent of the room revenue excluding tax for a booking made
by a registered travel agent through a contracted channel. Commission is not
payable on food and beverage, on other ancillary spend, or on tax.

Commission is payable only where the agent's IATA or registration number is
recorded on the reservation at the time of booking. A claim made after departure
against a booking that carried no agent reference is not paid, and this is
stated in the agent agreement.

Commission is not payable on a no-show, on a room withdrawn within the
withdrawal window, on a complimentary or house use room, or on a room sold under
the GOV code.

Commission is settled monthly, within thirty days of the month end, against a
statement issued by us rather than an invoice raised by the agent. Disputes are
raised within sixty days of the statement and are not entertained afterwards.

## 7. Supplier onboarding and due diligence

A supplier is engaged only after registration, which requires the constitution
documents, the GST registration, the PAN, the bank details verified by a
penny-drop test, and the licences required for the category of supply. Bank
details are verified independently of the email that supplied them, because an
intercepted email changing bank details is the most common fraud in this
industry.

Food suppliers additionally provide the FSSAI licence, the last two third-party
audit reports and the product specifications. Labour-supplying contractors
provide evidence of provident fund and employee state insurance registration and
monthly proof of remittance for the staff deployed with us.

A supplier is reviewed annually against price, quality, delivery reliability and
responsiveness, scored by the department that uses them rather than by
purchasing alone.

No member of staff accepts a gift, hospitality or a personal benefit from a
supplier or a prospective supplier beyond a nominal item of negligible value.
Anything received is declared in the gifts register within seven days. A
supplier offering a personal inducement is removed from the panel.

## 8. Laundry and linen supply

Linen laundry is contracted to Sparkle Linens Private Limited under contract
reference SL-2026-114, running from 1 April 2026 to 31 March 2028, with a single
option to extend by twelve months exercisable by us on ninety days' notice.

Collection is at 07:00 daily including Sundays and public holidays. Finished
linen is returned within twenty-four hours of collection. A return between
twenty-four and thirty-six hours is recorded as a late return; beyond thirty-six
hours the matter is escalated to procurement and a service credit of two per
cent of the monthly charge is applied per occurrence.

Rates are fixed per kilogram by category for the first twelve months. Thereafter
they are reviewed once annually and may not rise by more than the increase in
the Consumer Price Index for the preceding twelve months, or five per cent,
whichever is lower.

Loss or damage in the contractor's care is settled at the depreciated
replacement value against the linen register, reconciled monthly. Persistent
loss beyond one per cent of items despatched in any month is a material breach
and is notified in writing.

## 9. Food and beverage supply terms

Fresh produce is contracted to Malabar Fresh Produce LLP under reference
MFP-2026-208, with daily delivery between 06:00 and 08:00. Dry goods and
groceries are contracted to Konkan Provisions Private Limited under reference
KPP-2026-041, with delivery twice weekly on Tuesday and Friday.

Meat, poultry and seafood are contracted per property to a locally approved
supplier, and the approval is renewed on each FSSAI audit rather than annually
by the calendar.

Price is fixed for the contract period for dry goods and reviewed fortnightly
for fresh produce against a published market index agreed at contracting. A
supplier proposing a price change outside the review cycle is declined and the
requirement is met from the panel.

A delivery refused on temperature, quality or date coding under the F&B
receiving standard is recorded against the supplier. Three refusals in a
calendar month trigger a formal review, and six trigger removal from the panel.

## 10. Payment terms and credit

Standard supplier payment terms are thirty days from the date of a correct
invoice. An invoice that does not carry a valid purchase order number is
returned rather than held, and the thirty days run from the corrected
resubmission.

Payment runs are on the fifteenth and the last working day of each month. A
supplier is not paid outside a payment run except where the failure to pay on
time was ours.

Credit extended to a corporate account requires a completed credit application,
two trade references and the approval of the general manager. The standard
credit limit is two hundred thousand rupees and the standard term is thirty days
from the date of invoice.

An account exceeding its limit or sixty days overdue is placed on stop, which
means new bookings are accepted only against prepayment. Placing an account on
stop is the financial controller's decision and is notified to the company
before it takes effect, not after a guest has been refused at the desk.

## 11. Procurement approval limits

A purchase up to twenty-five thousand rupees is approved by the department head.
Up to two hundred thousand rupees requires the financial controller. Up to ten
lakh rupees requires the general manager. Above ten lakh rupees requires the
regional director and a board note where the item is capital.

Three written quotations are obtained for any purchase above fifty thousand
rupees. Where three cannot be obtained, the reason is recorded and the single
source is approved one level above the ordinary limit.

A purchase is never split into parts to bring it below an approval threshold.
Splitting is treated as falsification of records under the disciplinary standard.

Capital expenditure is approved against the annual capital plan. An item not in
the plan requires a separate business case regardless of value, and emergency
capital spend is ratified within seven days rather than absorbed.

## 12. Contract renewal, escalation and exit

Every contract carries a renewal date in the contract register, and the register
is reviewed monthly by the financial controller. A contract approaching its
renewal date is reviewed ninety days ahead so that a decision is made rather
than a renewal happening by default.

Automatic renewal clauses are accepted only where the notice period to prevent
renewal is ninety days or less. A contract that renews automatically on a longer
notice period is renegotiated before signature.

Price escalation is capped at the Consumer Price Index or five per cent,
whichever is lower, and is applied once annually on the contract anniversary. An
escalation claimed mid-term without a contractual basis is declined in writing
and the supplier is asked to invoice at the contracted rate.

On exit, the supplier returns our property and data, provides a final
reconciliation within thirty days, and cooperates with the incoming supplier for
a transition period of up to sixty days. These obligations are written into
every contract at signature rather than negotiated at the point of leaving,
which is the point at which we have the least leverage.
