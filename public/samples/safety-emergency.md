# Safety and Emergency Response Plan

Sanchari Hotels — all properties. Effective 1 April 2026. This plan is
rehearsed, not filed.

## 1. Fire alarm activation and first response

On activation, the panel indicates the zone. The security officer at the panel
announces the zone over the staff channel and the nearest trained member of
staff investigates within three minutes, taking a radio and the zone plan.

The investigator reports one of three findings: no fire, in which case the
alarm is silenced and reset by the duty manager and the cause recorded; a fire
that is small, contained and within the capacity of a single extinguisher; or a
fire that is not. The third finding requires no further assessment and no
telephone call for permission — the investigator calls the evacuation on the
radio and the fire service is called immediately.

The fire service is called on every activation that is not confirmed as a false
alarm within three minutes. A call is never delayed to avoid the embarrassment
of calling them out, and no member of staff at any level may instruct that a
call be held.

The duty manager takes charge at the panel and does not leave it. The panel is
where the incident is understood; a manager walking the building is a manager
who cannot see the second zone activate.

## 2. Evacuation and assembly

On the evacuation signal, every member of staff moves to their allocated
evacuation role. Guests are directed to the nearest safe staircase and never to
a lift. Staff sweep their allocated area, knocking and calling, closing doors
behind them, and mark the area swept on the sweep board at the exit.

The assembly point is the front car park at Sanchari Riverside and Sanchari
Residency, and the upper terrace garden at Sanchari Heights. Alternate assembly
points are published on the evacuation notice in every room and are used where
the primary point is affected by smoke or by the fire service's own operations.

The front desk brings the arrivals list, the in-house guest list and the staff
attendance record to the assembly point. The duty manager confirms every room
has been swept before reporting to the senior fire officer, and reports honestly
where a room has not been swept and why — a wrong assurance sends firefighters
past a room that needs them.

Nobody re-enters the building until the senior fire officer permits it. This
applies to staff retrieving records or cash without exception.

## 3. Extinguisher selection and use

Water extinguishers are used on wood, paper, textiles and furnishings only.
Carbon dioxide is used on electrical equipment and, at closer range, on
flammable liquids. Dry powder is used on flammable liquids and gases. Wet
chemical is used on cooking oils and fats, and is the only agent used on a fryer
fire.

Water is never used on an electrical fire or on burning oil. Water on burning
oil produces a violent eruption of flame, which is the single most common cause
of serious injury to hotel staff.

A member of staff attempts to fight a fire only where they are trained, the fire
is smaller than themselves, they have a clear escape route behind them, and the
alarm has already been raised. If the first extinguisher does not put it out,
they leave. There is no second attempt.

Extinguisher training is given at induction and repeated annually with a live
discharge, because an extinguisher is the only safety equipment we ask staff to
use in an emergency having never used one.

## 4. Medical emergency and first aid

At least two trained first aiders are on duty at all times, and the roster
names them. The list of first aiders on the current shift is displayed at the
security desk and at the staff entrance.

On a medical emergency, the switchboard is called first and states the location
and the nature of the emergency. The switchboard calls the ambulance service
and then the duty manager. A member of staff attends immediately and does not
wait for the first aider to arrive before starting to help.

First aid boxes are located at the front desk, the staff canteen, the kitchen,
the engineering workshop, the housekeeping desk, the poolside and the gymnasium.
Contents are checked monthly against the published list and after every use.
An automated external defibrillator is held at the front desk, checked weekly,
and its location is covered in induction for every member of staff regardless of
department.

We do not administer medication of any kind, including paracetamol, to a guest.
Where a guest asks, the duty manager arranges a doctor. This holds however
trivial the request appears.

## 5. Death of a guest on the property

The switchboard is informed and calls the duty manager, who attends personally.
Nothing in the room is touched, moved or removed, and the room is secured with
the corridor kept clear.

The police and a medical practitioner are called. The general manager is
informed at any hour. Where the guest is a foreign national, the consulate is
informed through the general manager, and the immigration authority is notified
in the same way as an arrival under the front office standard.

No member of staff speaks to the press, to another guest, or on any public
channel. All enquiries go to the general manager. The names of the deceased and
of the guests in adjacent rooms are not disclosed to anyone other than the
police.

Staff who attended are relieved from duty for the remainder of the shift on full
pay and are offered counselling. This is offered rather than waited for, and
taking it is never recorded against the member of staff.

## 6. Bomb threat and suspicious articles

A person receiving a threat by telephone keeps the caller talking, does not
interrupt, and completes the threat checklist held at every telephone: the exact
words used, the time, the caller's voice and manner, any background sound, and
whether a location or a time was stated. The line is not disconnected at the
end of the call.

The duty manager and the police are informed immediately. The decision to
evacuate is the police's advice and the general manager's decision, and it is
taken on the basis of the checklist rather than on instinct.

**Radios and mobile telephones are not used within one hundred metres of a
suspected device.** Staff search their own familiar areas for anything out of
place, because they know what belongs there and the police do not; nothing found
is touched, moved or covered.

Where evacuation is ordered, guests are moved to the alternate assembly point
rather than the primary one, since the primary point is close to the building
and may be within a hazard zone.

## 7. Flooding and water ingress

On discovering water ingress, the immediate action is to isolate the electrical
supply to the affected area at the distribution board and then to stop the
source. Electricity is isolated before anyone enters standing water, without
exception.

Guest rooms affected are vacated and the guests are moved with their belongings
by staff, at our cost, with an upgrade where one is available. Belongings damaged
are recorded with photographs the same day and settled by the general manager
rather than referred to an insurer before the guest has been answered.

Lifts are recalled to the ground floor and taken out of service where water has
entered a shaft or a machine room. Basement plant rooms are checked first during
heavy rain, since they flood before anything visible does.

Sandbags, wet vacuums and pumps are held at each property and their location is
covered in induction. Stock is checked before the monsoon each year and the
check is recorded.

## 8. Earthquake and structural events

During shaking, staff and guests take cover under a solid table or against an
internal wall away from glass, and remain there until the shaking stops. Nobody
runs for an exit while the building is moving, and nobody uses a lift at any
point.

After the shaking stops, the building is evacuated to the assembly point by the
staircase, and staff sweep as they leave under section 2. The building is not
re-entered until it has been inspected by a structural engineer, however
undamaged it appears.

The chief engineer isolates gas and checks for the smell of gas, for water
leaks, for cracks at column and beam junctions, and for damage to the lift
guides and the staircases, and reports to the general manager.

A guest who is injured is treated under section 4. A guest who is uninjured but
distressed is not left alone at the assembly point; a member of staff is
allocated to remain with them.

## 9. Power failure

Emergency lighting operates on failure and the generator picks up load within
fifteen seconds. Where the generator does not start, the chief engineer attends
the plant room and the duty manager posts staff at every staircase and at the
lift lobbies on each floor.

Lifts on the emergency supply return to the ground floor and open. Lifts not on
the emergency supply may hold passengers, so every lift is checked by
telephone and by opening the landing door where necessary, floor by floor, and
the check is reported to the duty manager as complete rather than assumed.

Guests are informed within ten minutes by a member of staff at the desk and on
each floor rather than by an announcement alone. Guests in a room without light
are attended individually and offered a torch, which is held at the housekeeping
floor station at four per floor.

Cold rooms and freezers are kept closed and their temperature is logged hourly
until supply is restored, and the food safety consequences under the F&B
standard are applied on restoration rather than on judgement.

## 10. Security incidents, theft and violence

A theft reported by a guest is recorded in full, the guest is offered the use of
a telephone and assistance in contacting the police, and the general manager is
informed. We do not tell a guest that we are not responsible, and we do not
discourage a police report, whatever the room safe notice says.

CCTV covering the relevant period is preserved immediately by the security
officer, before the routine overwrite reaches it. Preservation is recorded with
the times preserved and by whom.

Where a person is behaving violently, staff do not intervene physically except
to protect a person from immediate harm. The police are called, the area is
cleared of guests, and the duty manager attends. A member of staff who is
assaulted is relieved from duty, taken for medical attention, and supported in
making a police report; the decision to report is theirs and is not made for
them by the property.

An incident involving a member of staff and a guest is recorded by both the
duty manager and the head of the department, separately, on the same day.

## 11. CCTV, access control and privacy

Cameras cover the entrances, the lobby, the corridors, the lift cars, the
service entrance, the cash points and the car park. **No camera is installed in
a guest room, a bathroom, a changing room, or the staff changing rooms**, and a
camera found in such a location is removed the same day and the matter reported
to the general manager as a serious incident.

Recordings are retained for thirty days and then overwritten, other than
material preserved for an incident under section 10 or required by the police,
which is held until the matter is closed.

Access to live and recorded material is restricted to the security officer on
duty, the security manager and the general manager. Every access to recorded
material is logged with the reason. Material is released to the police against a
written request and to nobody else, including a guest asking to view footage of
themselves, who is directed to make the request through the police.

Signage stating that the premises are monitored is displayed at every entrance
and at the entrance to every monitored area.

## 12. Emergency contacts and the call tree

The emergency contact list is displayed at the security desk, the front desk and
the engineering workshop, and is reviewed monthly by the duty manager, who
telephones two numbers at random to confirm they are current.

The order of calling on a major incident is: emergency services first, then the
general manager, then the chief engineer, then the head of security, then the
regional operations head. The list is followed in order and calling continues
down it rather than stopping at the first person who does not answer.

Every member of staff carries the internal emergency number on their name badge.
The switchboard answers it in preference to any other call, including a call in
progress with a guest.

A drill is held quarterly at each property, at least one of which each year is
held at night with the night team, and the time to full evacuation is recorded
and reviewed. A drill that is not timed has not been evaluated.
